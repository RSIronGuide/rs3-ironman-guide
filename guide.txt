# Setup
- Make Female Character (required to complete Recruitment Drive, you can change after, quest happens pretty soon in guide)
- Finish Tutorial Island
* Great site for tracking your progression: https://thersguide.com/
- You can right click the World Map and adjust the skybox/filter settings to your liking
- Press Escape, search "shift" and make sure Shift-click to drop items is turned ON
- In the settings tab search "notifications", uncheck everything in "Broadcast - Global/Broadcasts - World" (so your chatbox doesn't get spammed)
* HCIM: be careful going AFK in starter areas, highwaymen will kill you
* Keep up with repeatable events:
  * Runespheres, Nemi Forest, Herby Werby, Troll Invasion, Memorial to Guthix, Circus, Penguin Hide and Seek, Familiarisation and Evil Tree are all a MUST early game. They will speed up your XP gains exponentially. If you aren't familiar with these minigames, learn them.
* The beginning of this guide is F2P until a certain point. Without membership you won't see certain items in the shops. Membership is recommended, but it's not necessary for the beginning.
* The Circus has 13 spawn locations, and where it spawns decides whether you can reach it. If it has a close spawn, go to it while we quest, or run to it now to unlock Surge. If it doesn't, we will finish Death Plateau soon and unlock Surge that way.
