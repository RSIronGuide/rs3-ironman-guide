# Setup
- Make Female Character (required to complete Recruitment Drive, you can change after, quest happens pretty soon in guide) 123
- Finish Tutorial Island
- You can right click the World Map and adjust the skybox/filter settings to your liking
- Press Escape, search "shift" make sure Shift-click to drop items is checked mark ON
- In the settings tab search "notifications", uncheck everything in "Broadcast - Global/Broadcasts - World" [So your chatbox doesn't spam]
- IF Hardcore ironman be careful going afk while in starter areas, highwaymen will kill you
- Keep up on Repeatable Events
  - Runespheres, Nemi Forest, Herby Werby, Troll Invasion, Memorial Guthix, Circus, Penguin Hide and Seek, Familiarisation, Evil tree are all a MUST early game. They will expedite your XP gains tremendously. If you aren't familiar with these mini games learn them.
  - The beginning of this guide is F2P until a certain point. Without members you won't see certain items in the shops FYI. I would advise members, but it's not necessary for the beginning.
    - The Circus has 13 spawn locations. Depending on spawn location will dictate if you can reach it. If it has a close spawn go to it when we quest or you can run to it now to unlock Surge. For those of us who don't have a close spawn we will finish Death Plateau soon and unlock surge that way.
